## tyt_intranet_birthday 

This module need of fields and models of odoo studio.

### List of dependencies by file

- The birthday_publication.py needs the fields: x_studio_empleado in res_users, x_studio_sitios0 in hr_employee.
- The hr_employee_views.xml needs the x_studio_sitios0.
- The portal_templates.xml needs the x_studio_empleado field.
